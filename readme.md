# HCM Lite — Client User Guide

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **HCM Lite**, a Human Capital Management system designed to help your organization manage employee information and attendance.

This guide explains how to log in, configure your company, set attendance rules, create employee and HR accounts, and use the attendance features.

## 2. User Roles and Permissions

HCM Lite supports different user roles. Each role has its own permissions.

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **Admin** | Manages company setup, office location, employees, HR access, and system configuration available to administrators. |
| **HR** | Manages employee and attendance-related activities available to HR users. |
| **Employee** | Views personal attendance information and checks in or out. |

**Important:** Users should only perform actions permitted by their assigned role.

## 3. How to Log In

Follow these steps to access HCM Lite.

1. Open the HCM Lite application using the website address provided by your administrator.
2. Enter your registered email address or username, depending on the login form.
3. Enter your password.
4. Click **Login** or **Sign In**.
5. After successful authentication, the system opens the dashboard or the page available to your role.

### If you cannot log in

- Check that your login details are correct.
- Confirm that your account has been created and activated.
- Check that you are using the correct application address.
- Contact your company administrator if you continue to experience problems.

Never share your password with another user.

## 4. Initial Company Setup — Admin

The administrator should complete the initial setup before employees begin using the attendance system.

### Step 1: Log in as Admin

Use the administrator account provided during the initial system setup.

### Step 2: Enter Company Details

Open the company settings or company management section, if available, and enter the information requested by the application.

Typical company information may include:

- Company name
- Company email address
- Contact number
- Office address
- Other business details supported by the system

Review the information for accuracy and save the changes.

**Note:** The exact company fields depend on the version of HCM Lite being used. If company registration or editing is not available in the current interface, contact the system administrator.

## 5. Configure Attendance Rules

Before employees start checking in, the administrator should confirm the attendance settings available in the application.

### Step 1: Review the Attendance Configuration

Open the attendance or settings section and review the available options.

### Step 2: Confirm the Company's Attendance Policy

The company should define its attendance policy before employees begin using the system. Depending on the features available, this may include:

- Official working hours
- Work start and end times
- Late arrival policy
- Break and overtime rules
- Attendance correction procedures

These are policy considerations; they may not all be configurable directly in the current HCM Lite interface.

### Step 3: Set the Office Location

If the system provides office-location settings:

1. Open the office-location section.
2. Enter the approved office latitude and longitude or use the location-selection method provided.
3. Save the location.
4. Verify that the saved coordinates correspond to the correct office.

The application may use the configured office location when validating employee attendance.

### Step 4: Review Attendance Access

Confirm that employee accounts can access the attendance page and that only authorized users can change administrative settings.

## 6. How to Create an Employee

An administrator or an authorized HR user should create employee accounts using the employee-management features available to their role.

1. Log in to HCM Lite.
2. Open the employee-management section.
3. Select the available option to add or create an employee.
4. Enter the employee information requested by the form.

Common employee details may include:

- Employee name
- Email address
- Employee ID
- Department or designation
- Contact details
- Assigned role

5. Review the entered information.
6. Save or submit the form.
7. Confirm that the employee appears in the employee list.
8. Provide the employee with the approved login instructions.

Only enter information requested by the application and authorized by company policy.

**Important:** Required fields, account-creation methods, and password setup depend on the current application. Follow the fields displayed on screen.

## 7. How to Add an HR User

An HR user should have the appropriate role assigned to their account.

1. Log in using an administrator account.
2. Open the user or employee-management section.
3. Choose the option to create a new user or employee, as supported by the application.
4. Enter the person's required details.
5. Select the **HR** role if the role-selection field is available.
6. Review the information and save the account.
7. Ask the HR user to sign in using their assigned credentials.
8. Confirm that the HR user can access the features permitted by their role.

**Security note:** Do not assign the Admin role to HR users unless they need administrator privileges and the company has authorized that access.

## 8. Employee Attendance — Check In and Check Out

Once an employee has an active account, they can use the attendance page.

### How to Check In

1. Open HCM Lite.
2. Log in using your employee account.
3. Navigate to the attendance page.
4. Allow location access if the browser requests it and your company's attendance process requires it.
5. Select **Check In**.
6. Wait for the system to confirm the result.
7. Verify that the attendance status or check-in time has been updated.

### How to Check Out

1. Open the attendance page.
2. Review your current attendance status.
3. Select **Check Out** when your work period ends.
4. Wait for the confirmation.
5. Verify that the check-out time or attendance status has been updated.

### Location Permission Troubleshooting

If HCM Lite requires GPS location and displays a location error:

1. Open the application in your browser.
2. Open the browser's site settings.
3. Set Location permission to **Allow**.
4. Ensure that your device's location services are enabled.
5. Reload the attendance page and try again.
6. If the error continues, contact your administrator or technical support.

Location permission alone does not guarantee success. The device, browser, and application must all be able to retrieve the location.

Do not enter false location information to bypass company attendance rules.

## 9. Viewing Attendance Information

Employees should open the attendance page to review the attendance information available to their accounts.

HR and Admin users should use the attendance features permitted by their roles to review employee attendance.

Depending on the implemented features, the application may show daily attendance status, check-in time, check-out time, or other attendance details.

If information is missing or incorrect, contact the authorized HR user or administrator.

## 10. Common Problems and Solutions

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Login fails | Verify your credentials and confirm that your account is active. |
| Employee cannot access a page | Ask the administrator to verify the assigned role and permissions. |
| Location permission denied | Allow location in the browser and check device location services. |
| Location cannot be retrieved | Reload the page, check your device's location services, and retry. |
| Check-in or check-out fails | Check the displayed error and confirm that you are logged in with an employee account. |
| Company settings cannot be edited | Confirm that you are logged in as Admin and that the feature is available. |
| Employee or HR account is missing | Ask an authorized administrator to verify the account and role. |

## 11. Security and Best Practices

- Keep passwords confidential.
- Use only your assigned account.
- Do not share administrator credentials with employees.
- Assign roles according to job responsibilities.
- Enter accurate employee and company information.
- Keep company settings and office-location information up to date.
- Log out when using a shared computer.
- Report suspicious access or incorrect attendance records to the administrator.
